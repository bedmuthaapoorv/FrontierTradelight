## Which days were actual trading days in this window?

### Takeaway
16–22 September 2026 contains only five NSE/BSE trading sessions: Wednesday 16, Thursday 17, Friday 18, Monday 21, and Tuesday 22. Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 are the weekend — not Saturday/Sunday the 20th/21st as the assignment's own phrasing suggested. This is a calendar-arithmetic finding, not something a single source states outright, so it is listed as an inference below rather than a cited fact.

### Cited Findings
- A MarketsMojo weekly-recap article ("Meesho Ltd Gains 0.72%: 4 Key Factors Driving the Week's Volatility," published 26 Sept 2026) presents a day-by-day close table that jumps directly from **21 September to 22 September** with no 19/20 entries, consistent with a weekend gap there — [MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/meesho-ltd-gains-072-4-key-factors-driving-the-weeks-volatility-4205385).
- A separate MarketsMojo intraday piece published **19 September 2026** ("Meesho Ltd is Rated Sell") refers to "the latest trading day" rather than describing same-day trading, consistent with 19 Sept being a non-trading Saturday and the article looking back at Friday 18 Sept's session — [MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stock-recommendation/meesho-ltd-is-rated-sell-4197922).

### Inferences
- 1 January 2026 falls on a Thursday (standard calendar fact); counting forward, 22 September 2026 is a Tuesday, making 16 Sept=Wed, 17 Sept=Thu, 18 Sept=Fri, 19 Sept=Sat, 20 Sept=Sun, 21 Sept=Mon, 22 Sept=Tue. So the real trading-day set for "16–22 Sept" is **{16, 17, 18, 21, 22}**, not {16,17,18,19,22} as suggested in the task brief.

### Gaps
- None — the weekend placement is confirmed by both calendar arithmetic and the shape of the MarketsMojo weekly table.

## Was the ₹216 (17 Sept) → ₹240 (22 Sept) move gradual or concentrated?

### Takeaway
The confirmed data strongly indicates the move was **heavily concentrated in the single session of 22 September** (+9.66%, ₹219.05→₹240.20), not a gradual climb across the week. The days before that (16–18, and even 21 Sept at only +1.74%) show the stock hovering in the ₹207–219 range per the (partly inferred) figures above, with the big jump happening on 22 Sept alone, followed by a multi-day pullback (23 Sept −1.60%, 24 Sept −1.48%, 25 Sept −6.87%).

### Cited Findings
- 21 Sept 2026 close ₹219.05 was only **+1.74%** versus the prior session — a modest move, not a big jump — [MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/meesho-ltd-gains-072-4-key-factors-driving-the-weeks-volatility-4205385).
- 22 Sept 2026 close ₹240.20 was **+9.66%** versus 21 Sept's close — by far the largest single-day move in the confirmed data set, driven by "a remarkable surge in trading volume... over 1.74 crore shares changing hands" and a reported ratings/institutional-interest catalyst — [MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/meesho-ltd-sees-exceptional-volume-surge-amid-strong-price-gains-4201355); [MarketsMojo weekly recap](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/meesho-ltd-gains-072-4-key-factors-driving-the-weeks-volatility-4205385).
- The rally reversed quickly afterward: 23 Sept −1.60% (₹236.35), 24 Sept −1.48% (₹232.85), 25 Sept −6.87% (₹216.85) — [MarketsMojo](https://www.marketsmojo.com/news/stocks-in-action/meesho-ltd-gains-072-4-key-factors-driving-the-weeks-volatility-4205385).

### Inferences
- Combining the confirmed 21→22 Sept jump with the (lower-confidence) back-calculated ≈₹215.3 figure for 18 Sept, the path across the window looks like: ~₹207–216 (16–18 Sept, largely flat/mixed) → ₹219.05 (21 Sept, +1.74%) → **₹240.20 (22 Sept, +9.66%, the concentrated spike)** → pullback through 25 Sept. This directly contradicts a "gradual climb" narrative and supports a "concentrated in one day" narrative.

### Gaps
- Without confirmed 16/17 Sept closes, it cannot be stated with certainty whether there was also a smaller build-up move earlier in the week (e.g., 16→17→18 Sept) — only that 21 Sept itself was not a big move and 22 Sept clearly was.
